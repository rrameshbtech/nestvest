Flow notes

A new destination, familiar paths.

  • Open the Kakeibo destination from the post-profile dashboard; amount-first expense capture remains available there and is not replaced.
  • The introduction can be skipped or revisited. Planning never depends on completing a tutorial.
  • Plan totals and weekly pacing validate before a review-and-confirm step. A failed save keeps the draft for retry.
  • Progress uses saved same-currency expenses; unassigned and other-currency amounts remain separate. Overspending appears as a negative balance and is named.
  • Reflection can be postponed. Saving and reopening the review are simulated in memory only.
  • Existing Phase 1 profile and Phase 2 expense interactions are referenced, not duplicated or changed.
